Neither syscall_cp.s nor clone.s moves the stack pointer, so the default rules
describe both and the generator only has to open and close the frame. The
"addi sp,sp,-n" form and register stores to the stack are handled anyway, for
the files that do use them and so the generator does not silently mistrack a
future change.

clone.s carries no end-of-stack marker and needs none: __clone appears once
per thread there today. Clearing the return-address register the way _start
does would not work here, because the child calls the thread function with
jalr, which sets it again.

Verified by building musl with this and reading the FDE coverage back out of
the resulting libc.so. No live backtrace was taken on this architecture.
