Neither syscall_cp.s nor clone.s moves r1: clone.s builds the child's frame in
r4, not in the stack pointer. The default rules therefore describe both, and
the generator only has to open and close the frame. The "stdu 1,-n(1)" and
"addi 1,1,n" forms are handled all the same, so that a file which does move r1
is not silently mistracked; crti.s and crtn.s use them today but declare no
.type,%function, so nothing is emitted there.

clone.s carries no end-of-stack marker and needs none: __clone appears once
per thread there today.

Verified by building musl with this and reading the FDE coverage back out of
the resulting libc.so. No live backtrace was taken on this architecture.
