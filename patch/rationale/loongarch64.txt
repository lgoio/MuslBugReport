syscall_cp.s has no prologue and returns through ra, so opening and closing
the frame is enough. clone.s zeroes the frame pointer in the child, which the
generator turns into .cfi_undefined for ra.

Note this port spells the type directive .type name,@function rather than
%function, like x86 and unlike the other ports touched here.

Verified by building musl with this and reading the FDE coverage back out of
the resulting libc.so. No live backtrace was taken on this architecture.
