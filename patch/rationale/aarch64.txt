syscall_cp.s has no prologue at all - __syscall_cp_asm and __cp_begin share an
address and the stub returns straight through x30 - so the default rules
already describe it and .cfi_startproc/.cfi_endproc are enough to produce the
FDE that is missing today.

Pre-indexed stp and post-indexed ldp are the only way the stack pointer moves
in this port; unlike ARM's stm, stp stores its operands in the order they are
written. clone.s saves to the new thread stack in x1 rather than to sp, and
its child zeroes the frame pointer, which the generator turns into
.cfi_undefined for x30.

Verified by building musl with this, replacing the container's libc and taking
a backtrace with plain gdb.
