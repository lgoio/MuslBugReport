=== generator variant - measured, not argued

tools/add-cfi.arm.awk and tools/add-cfi.aarch64.awk were written after
tools/add-cfi.x86_64.awk and dropped into an otherwise untouched musl
1.2.6 tree. No .s file was edited. configure then finds them and turns
ADD_CFI on by itself, which puts every arm/aarch64 .s file through the
generator - 13 files per architecture, not the 2 that carry the bug.

Build: Alpine 3.24, gcc 15.2.0, CFLAGS="-g -O2", both architectures.
Then the container libc was replaced and the reproducer backtraced with
plain gdb, exactly as in the hand-written run.

---------------- verdict ----------------

Both architectures build and every sleeping thread unwinds to its entry
point. The generator does the job it was written for.

It cannot finish clone.s. Terminating a thread stack means marking the
return address undefined in the child path, and nothing in the assembly
says which path that is - it is knowledge about what the function means,
not about what it does. On arm __clone therefore still repeats until the
backtrace limit; on aarch64 it stops, but with "corrupt stack?" instead
of a clean end.

The hand-written patch ends both cleanly, because it carries that one
directive. Either way clone.s needs it, so the two are not alternatives.
